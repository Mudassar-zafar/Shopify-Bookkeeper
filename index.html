The four things that make Mood's books tricky, and how we handle each

  • Three revenue streams (retail, subscription, wholesale) each settle and get taxed differently. We keep them separate and correct, not lumped together.
  • Sales tax across many states, with wholesale treated as resale-exempt. We track what was collected in each state so filing is ready, not a scramble.
  • Inventory and COGS on 155 products plus bundles. We book cost against each sale so your margin is real, not a guess.
  • Card payouts, fees and refunds that never equal the sale amount. We reconcile the processor deposit to the bank to the penny.

How we keep it reliable, every week and month

Clean books are a rhythm, not a year-end rescue. Here is the routine we run so nothing piles up and you are never guessing where things stand.

Weekly Keep it current

  • Categorize new orders across retail, subscription and wholesale.
  • Match processor payouts to the bank and flag anything that does not.
  • Record refunds and any chargebacks, and update inventory received.

Month-end Close it properly

  • Reconcile every payout and bank line, and every card account.
  • Book COGS and adjust inventory value for what sold and what came in.
  • Prepare sales tax collected by state, filing-ready, wholesale kept exempt.

Each month you get A clear picture

  • Profit and loss, cash position, and inventory value.
  • Gross margin by channel, so you see which stream actually pays.
  • The exact sales tax to remit, and where.

Built for how a hemp business actually runs

  • Wholesale booked as resale-exempt, with certificates kept on file.
  • Per-state tax and shipping rules tracked as they change.
  • High-risk processor payouts and fees reconciled, not assumed.
  • These are hemp products under the 2018 Farm Bill, not state-licensed marijuana, so we keep your books organized the standard way and fully aligned with how your CPA treats them, no surprises at tax time.
